# Albugen

One album a week, drawn from Apple Music. Tap the cover to listen, rate it out of 10 and star your favourite tracks.

## Install on your phone

Albugen is a home-screen web app (PWA). It has to be served over HTTPS, for example with GitHub Pages:

1. In the repo on GitHub, go to **Settings → Pages**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, pick the branch and `/ (root)`, then save.
2. Open the Pages URL in **Safari** on your iPhone.
3. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

It then opens full-screen with its own icon. Android (Chrome) offers **Install app** from the menu.

Ratings and picks are stored on the device the app is installed on.
